# 2026-W42 (12–18 Oct 2026)

## Done — three model milestones
1. **C1 rate adaptation measured** ([results/c1_rate_adaptation](../../results/c1_rate_adaptation/README.md)). One mother code with puncture/shorten masks reaches f = 1.38–1.87 over Q = 2–6% and cannot serve Q = 1% at all. Puncturing is reliable only up to ~10% of positions.
2. **L2 bit-true model and E2** ([results/e2_quantisation](../../results/e2_quantisation/README.md)). The spec's fixed-point defaults show **zero quantisation loss** against the float model, and sit exactly on the cliff: W_MSG 5 or W_APP 6 collapse, 7 bits gains nothing. Spec section 2.1 statuses moved from TBC to confirmed.
3. **Numba kernels, bit-exact with the reference** ([results/bench_decoder](../../results/bench_decoder/README.md)). 5–21× faster; at n = 65,536 a 10⁵-frame point costs about 45 minutes on one core, so publishable sample sizes are now affordable.

## Decisions forced by measurement
- **Two mother codes, not one** (docs/06 §2a, docs/03 open items). The single-code assumption in REQ-FUN-03 does not survive the puncture budget.
- The puncturable subset must be **larger than the punctured set**: puncturing every heavy column strips the graph and fails, while puncturing part of it helped (FER 0.96 → 0.24 at 3%).
- W_APP = 7 suffices; the spec's 8 carries one bit of margin worth revisiting when the BRAM budget is tight.
